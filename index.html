Transit Photometry Analysis

Kepler & TESS High-Precision Modeling

Problem: Degeneracy between limb darkening and planetary radius (Rp/Rs).
Method: Bayesian MCMC inference with Quadratic Law (u1, u2) using Claret (2017) priors.
Targets: Validated on HD 209458b, Kepler-186f, and TOI-700 d.
Result: Reduced radius bias by 5.1% ± 1.2% for high-impact (b > 0.8) transits.
